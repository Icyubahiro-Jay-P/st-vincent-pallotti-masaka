# Saint Vincent Pallotti School Masaka

> Saint Vincent Pallotti School Masaka is a Pallottine Missionary Sisters school in Masaka, Kigali, Rwanda - run by the Our Lady of Kibeho Region. Bilingual English/French (cookie-driven, no locale prefix), covering Day Care (3 months - 2 yrs) through Secondary (S1-S6), Cambridge and National curricula, Special Needs Education, and TVET vocational training. Motto: Strive Beyond (Caritas Christi urget nos).

## Academic Programs

- [Day Care](https://www.stvincentpallottimasaka.com/academics/day-care): Warm, secure nursery for 3 months - 2 years with Montessori-inspired play
- [Kindergarten](https://www.stvincentpallottimasaka.com/academics/kindergarten): Play-based early learning for 3 - 5 years, preparing for Primary One
- [Special Needs Education](https://www.stvincentpallottimasaka.com/academics/special-needs): Individualised, inclusive support available at every level from Day Care to Secondary
- [Cambridge Primary](https://www.stvincentpallottimasaka.com/academics/cambridge-primary): Internationally benchmarked Cambridge Primary in English, Mathematics and Science (P1-P6)
- [National Nursery & Primary](https://www.stvincentpallottimasaka.com/academics/national-primary): Rwandan competence-based curriculum across Nursery N1 to P6
- [National Secondary](https://www.stvincentpallottimasaka.com/academics/national-secondary): O-Level and A-Level pathways (S1-S6) for national examinations and university entry
- [TVET / Vocational](https://www.stvincentpallottimasaka.com/tvet): Post-S3 trade certification across five workshops (also listed under TVET)

## TVET Trades

- [Welding & Fabrication](https://www.stvincentpallottimasaka.com/tvet): Metal joining, fabrication and workshop safety toward RTB certification
- [Tailoring & Fashion Design](https://www.stvincentpallottimasaka.com/tvet): Garment construction, pattern-making and small-business skills
- [Hairdressing & Beauty](https://www.stvincentpallottimasaka.com/tvet): Modern salon techniques, client care and beauty business basics
- [Carpentry & Joinery](https://www.stvincentpallottimasaka.com/tvet): Furniture-making, structural woodwork and precision tool use
- [Culinary Arts](https://www.stvincentpallottimasaka.com/tvet): Professional kitchen practice, food safety and hospitality skills
